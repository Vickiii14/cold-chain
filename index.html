Pipeline

From raw sensor data to a clear decision

Every reading flows through the same six stages, all running on the ESP32 itself.

1

Sense

Temperature, humidity and motion are sampled continuously.

2

Timestamp

Each important event is logged into the exposure history.

3

Accumulate

Severity × duration × repetition builds a dynamic risk score.

4

Estimate

Risk becomes a remaining safe-life percentage.

5

Adapt

Sampling speeds up or slows down with the risk level.

6

Decide

A plain recommendation: continue, monitor, inspect…

Cumulative risk algorithm

A product remembers every bad minute

A fixed threshold forgets. Our engine integrates how far, how long and how often conditions leave the safe band, so a short spike and a long drift are treated differently.

// every sample, Δt in seconds
excursion = max(T − T_max, T_min − T, 0)
risk += excursion · (Δt/60) · k₁ · (1 + 0.25·repeats)
risk += max(shock_g − 1.8, 0) · k₂
condition = max(0, 100 − risk)

Features

Built like a product, priced like a prototype

The research focus: exposure-based remaining-life estimation combined with risk-driven adaptive sensing.

🌡️

Exposure-based life

Condition percentage is computed from the product's entire history, not a single reading.

⚡

Adaptive sampling

Long intervals when safe to save battery; rapid sampling when conditions turn critical.

🧠

Edge processing

Risk and decisions are computed on-device, so monitoring continues without internet.

📳

Shock detection

MPU6050 flags drops and rough handling, adding to the risk score.

📟

Local OLED

Temperature, humidity, risk level, life estimate and status — right on the package.

☁️

Cloud dashboard

Firebase-backed live charts and history for remote monitoring and analysis.
